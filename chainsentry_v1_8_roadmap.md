# 🗺️ ChainSentry v1.8.0: The Taproot Channels Update
This upcoming version transitions ChainSentry's monitoring capabilities from legacy P2WSH channels to Simple Taproot Channels.
1. **MuSig2 Integration:** Key aggregation and partial signature verification.
2. **PTLC Routing:** Unlocking advanced privacy for multi-hop payments.
3. **P2TR Gossip:** Parsing Taproot-specific channel announcement messages.
